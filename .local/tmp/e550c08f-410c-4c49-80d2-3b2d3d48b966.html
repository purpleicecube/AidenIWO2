Iowa Welcome Site – Structure & Design Guidelines

\n

Apple‑inspired visual language with clean layout, ample whitespace, Helvetica/Arial typography, subtle gradients, and high‑resolution imagery.

\n
\n
\n
\n

Site Structure (Five Pages)

\n
\n
\n\n

Home

\n

Welcome visitors with a full‑width hero, brief tagline, and primary call‑to‑action linking to Products and Solutions.

\n
\n
\n\n

About

\n

Company mission, values, and a short story about Iowa’s heritage, presented in concise sections.

\n
\n
\n\n

Products

\n

Grid layout of product cards with high‑resolution images, brief specs, and “Learn More” links.

\n
\n
\n\n

Solutions

\n

Explains how products integrate into real‑world scenarios, using icons and short paragraphs.

\n
\n
\n\n

Contact

\n

Simple contact form, map embed, and quick links to support channels.

\n
\n
\n
\n

Apple‑Style Visual Guidelines

\n
    \n
  • Layout: Grid‑based, generous whitespace, left‑aligned content blocks.
    • \n
  • Typography: Primary font Helvetica, fallback Arial; headings 28‑44 pt, body 16‑18 pt.
    • \n
  • Color palette: Neutral background #F5F5F7, dark text #0A0A0A, accent #007AFF for links and buttons.
    • \n
  • Gradients: Subtle linear gradients (e.g., top‑to‑bottom #FFFFFF → #F0F0F5) used in hero sections.
    • \n
  • Imagery: High‑resolution placeholder images from picsum.photos, aspect ratios 16:9 for hero, 4:3 for cards.
    • \n
  • Buttons & Links: Rounded corners (6 pt), minimal drop‑shadow, hover transition to accent color.
    • \n
  • Accessibility: Sufficient color contrast, ARIA labels on navigation, alt text for all images, focus outlines visible.
    • \n
