\n\n\n\n\nIowa Site – Preview Instructions\n\n\n\n
Preview Iowa Site
\n
To preview the Iowa website in the sandbox environment, you have two options:
\n
    \n  
  1. Open any of the HTML files (e.g., home.html, about.html, features.html, pricing.html, contact.html) directly in your browser.
    2. \n  
  2. Drag the entire folder containing the site files into the sandbox iframe. The sandbox will automatically serve the files.
    3. \n
\n
A convenience index.html is provided that redirects to the Home page:
\n
<!DOCTYPE html>\n<html>\n<head>\n  <meta http-equiv=\"refresh\" content=\"0; url=home.html\">\n  <title>Iowa Site</title>\n</head>\n<body>\n  Redirecting to <a href=\"home.html\">Home</a>...\n</body>\n</html>\n
\n
All site files (the five HTML pages, any assets, and this README) are packaged in iowa-site.zip. Use the one‑click preview link below to load the archive in the sandbox:
\n
