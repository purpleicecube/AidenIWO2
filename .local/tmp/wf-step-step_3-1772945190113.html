Arts & Traditions

Iowa Culture

A rich tapestry of literary heritage, world-class arts, vibrant festivals, and the enduring traditions of heartland communities.

Literature

Iowa Writers' Workshop

The world's most prestigious creative writing program has shaped generations of American literature, producing 17 Pulitzer Prize winners.

Festivals

Iowa State Fair

One of America's oldest and largest state fairs, drawing over a million visitors each August with competitions, concerts, and legendary food.

Visual Arts

Grant Wood Country

The rolling hills of eastern Iowa inspired American Gothic and a regionalist art movement that defined a uniquely American aesthetic.

Music

Hometown of Legends

Iowa gave the world Buddy Holly's last concert, Glenn Miller's birthplace, and a thriving contemporary music scene in every city.

Heritage

Indigenous Roots

Iowa's name honors the Ioway people. The state preserves rich Native American heritage through museums, mounds, and living traditions.

Cuisine

Farm-to-Table Roots

Iowa's culinary scene celebrates its agricultural abundance with farm-fresh restaurants, craft breweries, and beloved local traditions.

A Legacy of the Land

1833

First Settlers Arrive

European-American settlers begin farming Iowa's rich black soil, recognizing its extraordinary agricultural potential.

1858

Iowa Agricultural College Founded

Now Iowa State University, it becomes the first land-grant college in the nation, pioneering agricultural science.

1930s

Hybrid Corn Revolution

Iowa researchers develop hybrid corn varieties that transform American agriculture and dramatically increase yields.

1970s

Soybean Expansion

Iowa diversifies its agricultural base with soybeans, becoming a global leader in oilseed production.

Today

Sustainable Innovation

Iowa farmers lead in precision agriculture, renewable energy, and sustainable practices while feeding billions worldwide.

Urban Life

Iowa's Cities

Vibrant urban centers where innovation meets Midwestern warmth — from the state capital to college towns and river cities.

Des Moines
The capital city — a rising star with world-class arts, a booming food scene, and the iconic Iowa State Capitol.
Cedar Rapids
The City of Five Seasons — home to the National Czech & Slovak Museum, vibrant arts district, and Czech Village.
Iowa City
A UNESCO City of Literature — home to the University of Iowa, the Writers' Workshop, and a thriving arts community.
Dubuque
Iowa's oldest city perches dramatically above the Mississippi, with Victorian architecture and the iconic Fenelon Place Elevator.
Davenport
Part of the Quad Cities, birthplace of jazz legend Bix Beiderbecke, with a lively riverfront and thriving arts scene.
Ames
A dynamic college town anchored by Iowa State University — innovation hub, Big 12 athletics, and vibrant student culture.
Low Cost of Living
Iowa consistently ranks among the most affordable states, with housing costs well below the national average.
Top-Ranked Schools
Iowa's public schools consistently rank among the nation's best, with high graduation rates and strong academic performance.
Safe Communities
Iowa cities regularly rank among America's safest, with strong community bonds and a high quality of life.
Friendly People
Iowa Nice is real — the state's welcoming culture and genuine hospitality make newcomers feel at home immediately.

Travel Guide

Visit Iowa

Everything you need to plan an unforgettable trip to the heart of America. From scenic byways to world-class events.

Getting Here

Des Moines International Airport offers direct flights from major hubs. Interstate highways I-80 and I-35 cross the state, making Iowa easily accessible by car.

Best Times to Visit

Late spring (May–June) for wildflowers and mild weather. Summer for the State Fair and outdoor festivals. Fall for stunning foliage and harvest season beauty.

Scenic Byways

Drive the Great River Road along the Mississippi, explore the Loess Hills National Scenic Byway, or follow the Lincoln Highway — America's first coast-to-coast road.

Music & Festivals

From the Iowa State Fair to the Bix Beiderbecke Jazz Festival, RAGBRAI cycling event, and countless local celebrations throughout the year.

Outdoor Adventures

Hike Backbone State Park, canoe the Upper Iowa River, bike the High Trestle Trail, or birdwatch at Saylorville Lake — Iowa's outdoors never disappoint.

Where to Stay

From boutique hotels in Des Moines' East Village to charming B&Bs in river towns, farm stays in the countryside, and full-service resorts at Iowa's lakes.
